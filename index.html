I. Who Are You

A name, and a few words on who this character is. You can leave the story blank and fill it in later.

II. The Six Stats

Every stat starts at 1 (that's 6 points already placed). You have a total budget of 9, so 3 points remain to distribute — and no stat may go above 2 at creation. You will be fragile at first; that is intended.
